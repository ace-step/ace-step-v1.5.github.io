[Intro]
[Tin whistle plays melody alone with ornamental cuts and rolls, guitar drone fades in beneath]

[Verse 1]
There was a house upon the hill
Where the sea wind bent the only tree
My grandmother would stand at the door
And name every boat that she could see
She knew them by their sails she said
The way you'd know a man by how he walks
Some come home heavy some come home light
And some don't come home at all

[Verse 2]
[Bodhrán enters with a soft rolling pulse]
She kept a candle on the sill
Not for God not for the dark
But for the one who left in April
When the herring still were running south
His name is in the churchyard now
Carved in stone that faces out to sea
She said the dead should have a view
It's the least that we can do

[Chorus]
[Fiddle enters with harmony line above the vocal]
And the water takes and the water gives
And the water doesn't explain itself
It just moves the way that grief does
Steady and ordinary and endless
And we stand on the shore
And we call it beautiful
Because what else can you call
A thing that large and that indifferent

[Verse 3]
I asked her once if she was lonely
She looked at me the way old women do
When the young say something very small
And expect it to fill the room
She said lonely is a word for people
Who still think the world owes them company
I just live here with the wind
And the wind has never disappointed me
