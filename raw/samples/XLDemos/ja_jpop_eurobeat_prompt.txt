An explosive, high-energy J-pop track with strong Eurobeat and trance influences. The song kicks off with a flurry of bright, arpeggiated synth leads and a driving four-on-the-floor drum machine beat. A powerful, clear female vocal soars over layers of supersaw synth chords and a punchy synth bassline. The arrangement is dynamic, building through verses into an anthemic, uplifting chorus where the vocals become more layered and impassioned. An instrumental break showcases a melodic synth lead before the track builds again to a final, powerful chorus and ends with a sharp, conclusive synth flourish.
