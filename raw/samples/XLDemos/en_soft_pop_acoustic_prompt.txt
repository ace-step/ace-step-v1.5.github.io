A gentle soft pop song with a warm intimate feel, centered on a fingerpicked acoustic guitar with a mellow cedar-top tone playing a simple repeating pattern with open string ringing. A male vocalist sings in a relaxed mid-range with a slightly breathy quality and a conversational delivery that sits close and quiet like talking to someone next to you on a couch. A soft pad synthesizer holds long warm chords beneath the guitar adding a subtle bed of warmth without drawing attention to itself. Light tambourine shakes gently on the choruses only. The arrangement stays small and close the entire way through, never building to anything loud, just staying in the pocket of a late evening living room conversation. Production is warm and intimate with the vocal mixed dry and upfront and a soft analog warmth across everything.
